Instrumental only, no vocals. An upbeat, fun, original 1980s funk groove at about 110 BPM: punchy slap bass, choppy wah-wah guitar, bright brass stabs, a drum machine with crisp handclaps and a light synth pad. Steady energy from the first second to the last, with no breakdown, no silence and no big ending, so it works as a continuous background bed under a voice-over.
