= Frequently Asked Questions
:toc:

== General

=== What is a Zap?

A Zap is a micro-payment of Sats (satoshis — the smallest unit of Bitcoin)
sent to a colleague as recognition. Zaps move between LNbits wallets over the
Bitcoin Lightning Network.

=== Is this real money?

Yes. Sats are real Bitcoin. Deployments typically fund wallets with small
allowances, but LNbits keys must always be treated as production credentials.

=== Can we call the reward something other than "Sats"?

Yes — an administrator can rename the reward label on the Settings tab. The
name is used by both the web tabs and the bot's replies.

== Usage

=== How do I send a Zap?

Message the bot `send zap` in Teams, pick up to three recipients (the card
says the limit; an administrator can change it), add a
message and an amount (1 up to your Allowance balance, at most 10,000; the
card refuses more before you can submit; with several recipients the total
is checked when you submit), and submit. The card updates in place with the
result and your remaining balance.

=== Why can I only zap three people at a time?

Teams gives the bot 15 seconds to answer a card, and each recipient is paid
one after another, so a card with more people could run out of time and show
you an error while the zaps were still going through. To thank more people,
send a second card.

=== Why did sending to a colleague fail?

Recipients must have interacted with the app at least once so their wallets
exist. Failed recipients are listed on the result card — ask them to message
the bot, then try again.

=== Where do received Zaps go?

Into your *Private* wallet (visible on the Wallet tab). Zaps you send are drawn
from your *Allowance* wallet. It receives a one-off starting balance when your
account is created, if your organisation has configured one. Recurring top-ups,
where configured, come from the LNbits allowance extension rather than the bot.

=== Can I withdraw my Sats?

`withdraw my zaps` is not yet implemented — it is on the roadmap.

== Technical

=== Which LNbits version is required?

LNbits 1.7 or higher (bank.knowall.ai runs 1.7.1 with mandatory 2FA). Zaplie
uses an ordinary account, `zaplie.service`, with the `lnurlp` and `allowance`
extensions enabled for it; it never logs in and never uses the admin Users
API. See `docs/LNBITS_MIGRATION.adoc`.

=== How are Teams users mapped to wallets?

By the Entra ID (AAD) object id, in Zaplie's own people directory
(`people.json`, NWC connections encrypted). `FetchUserMiddleware` adds the person
on their first bot interaction; their Allowance wallet belongs to
`zaplie.service`, and they receive zaps in their own Private wallet through a
Lightning address they register on the portal's Wallet page. Zaplie spends
from Allowance wallets over Nostr Wallet Connect, with a budget LNbits
enforces, and holds no wallet key.

=== How do I report a security issue?

Privately — see `SECURITY.md` (support@knowall.ai or Nostr DM). Do not open a
public GitHub issue.
